# Arena

Use competing attempts to settle an open design or implementation question.

1. State one artifact contract and concrete comparison criteria before generating
   candidates. Use the same task and grounding for every candidate.
2. Create at least two distinct approaches in isolated output locations. Use
   native delegates when available and permitted by [runtime](../runtime.md).
   Otherwise explore sequentially and identify the lack of independent authorship.
3. Judge completed candidates against the same criteria. Where an independent
   reviewer is available, give it neutral candidate labels. Do not claim blinded
   or multi-model evaluation when the setup does not provide it.
4. Read every candidate yourself. Choose a base, explain why, and take useful
   ideas from the others only when they fit the chosen structure.
5. [Verify](verify.md) the combined artifact. Evidence for a candidate does not
   automatically validate the synthesis. Record dropouts and untested dimensions.
