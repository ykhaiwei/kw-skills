# Investigation

1. State the question and what evidence could answer it. Keep the work read-only.
2. Trace the relevant behavior with [how](../workflows/how.md). Use
   [why](../workflows/why.md) when the question concerns intent or tradeoffs.
3. Test uncertain claims with a small read-only probe when source is insufficient.
   Mark facts, inferences, and unresolved questions distinctly.
4. Explain the answer with source pointers, evidence, and the practical implication.
   For a discussion, compare credible alternatives and recommend one. Stop at the
   discussion unless implementation is requested.

Reply with the answer first, then the evidence and remaining uncertainty.
